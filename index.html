Abstract

SARA surgical agentic system overview. Given a nominal graph GN composed of reusable surgical skills, SARA proactively anticipates failure modes, synthesizes observable verifiers, and plans corresponding recovery chains to construct an augmented directed graph GA. Through iterative self-improvement and validation within a Newton physics-based dVRK sandbox, the augmented graph is deployed to the physical dVRK platform for resilient task execution.

The method

Anticipation before execution.
Recovery by construction.

Task instructions and reusable skills define the nominal graph. SARA augments and refines that graph through rehearsal before physical deployment.
